WHO WE ARE

A practical approach to print production.

Sarasavi Offset Printers began operations in 2003. Our work covers printing, laminating, die cutting and pasting, supporting customers who need printed materials and packaging.

We also work with print businesses through Sarasavi Colour Separations, established in 2017, and Sarasavi Printing Need Suppliers, which provides printing materials and supplies.
