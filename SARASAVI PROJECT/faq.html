Frequently asked questions.

Useful information before sending your print requirements.

How can I request a quotation?

Use the quotation form to share product type, quantity, dimensions and finishing requirements. We will need your details before pricing can be discussed.

Can I upload my artwork?

Yes. Our quotation form accepts PDF, PNG and JPG files up to 4 MB when run through the included Python server. Larger or specialist artwork can be arranged directly by email.

Do you offer packaging and labels?

The website showcases printed cartons, product packaging and labels. Please contact us to confirm suitable materials and specifications for your order.

How long does printing take?

Timing depends on the quantity, material, artwork and production requirements. Ask for an estimate when submitting your quotation request.

Can I order a sample?

Send an enquiry with the product and sample type you need. Availability and any sample cost must be confirmed by the business.

How can I check my order?

For requests created using the quotation form, use the reference and PIN on the tracking page. Orders handled outside this website should be checked with the business.
